Frequently asked questions

How do streaks work?

Each goal has a frequency (daily, weekly, or custom). Complete it within the required window to extend your streak. Missing a window resets the streak unless you use a Streak Rescue.

How does Health Auto-Tracking work?

When you create a fitness-related goal, you can enable auto-tracking so Streak Bubble reads matching data from Apple Health or Google Health Connect and marks the goal complete automatically. You can turn this on or off per goal, and revoke the underlying permission at any time from your phone's Health settings. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Who can see my Streak Album photos?

You choose the audience for each photo or video: Private (only you), Friends, or Public (visible in the Discover feed). You can change the visibility of any item at any time.

How do I add a friend or accountability buddy?

Search for a username from the Friends tab to send a request. Once connected, you can optionally assign them as an accountability buddy on specific goals, with buddy approval and stakes.

How do I cancel Streak Bubble Plus?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID (Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or your Google Play account (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions), not within the app.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account in the app, or see our Account Deletion page for full details.

How do I report a user or piece of content?

Tap the "..." menu on a user's profile or a piece of content and select Report or Block. Our team reviews all reports against our Community Guidelines.
